# BizNexus - Business Ecosystem

BizNexus is a modern and responsive Business Management Dashboard designed for small businesses and startups.

It helps business owners manage their complete business from one place.

### Features
- Dashboard with Business Health Score
- Employee Management
- Customer Management (CRM)
- Project Management
- Task Management
- Inventory Management with Low Stock Alerts
- Business Analytics
- Notifications System
- Dark Mode / Light Mode
- Settings & Profile Management

### Technologies Used
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

### How to Use
Just open `index.html` in your browser and explore the dashboard.
